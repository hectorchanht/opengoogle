OpenGoogle

Dawn's search-and-ranking app — a PageRank-lineage engine, open and inspectable, first turned inward on the Dawn canon itself, then outward.

Founding — initialized, first release in preparation

What it is

Its engine is the PageRank lineage learned on founding night (Brin & Page mechanics + TrustRank seeds + edge policy + freshness as a separate signal), rebuilt Dawn-style: open, inspectable, and graded by the three hands — machine score, editor curation, public handpicks.

Founding assets already exist — assemble, don't restart: the Google-ranking research full text (vault: OpenPaper shelf), the interactive PageRank lab page (a working power-iteration + random-surfer demonstrator), and the MD auto-grader sibling mechanism.

Standing rule: no claim before material — “searches the Dawn DB” is a claim that lands only when the inward engine passes its acceptance tests.

End Goal

A search-and-ranking engine Dawn can inspect end to end: first the Canon Navigator's engine room — ranked retrieval over the vault and canon, with provenance on every result — then the same engine offered publicly as a Dawn product.

Roadmap

  1. Turn 1 — Inward first: search and rank the Dawn DB itself. Acceptance ties to the Canon Navigator's own test （注意力閘 ≡ “attention gate” → same node), plus ranked retrieval over the vault/canon with provenance on every result.
  2. Turn 2 — Outward second: the same engine offered publicly as a Dawn product — scope and surface to be grilled when the inward engine verifies. Do not build outward before inward proves itself (dogfood gate).
